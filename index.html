Three lifting days, one long run, and an optional interval day on Tuesday or Wednesday. Four weeks: calibrate, load it, push, test. Tap any day for the session and why it's written that way.

Log everything in Hevy and Strava

Every weight in here is an estimate — I built them all from one number, your 225 × 4 bench. Log your lifts in Hevy and your runs in Strava and I can stop guessing: next month's weights come from what you actually did, not from arithmetic. Until then, trust how the set feels over what the sheet says.

Full range, every rep

All the way down, all the way up, on everything. The stretched bottom of a rep is where most of the growth comes from, so half-reps with more weight on the bar buy you nothing — they just make the number look better. If a weight stops you reaching full range, it's too heavy. And if something hurts, take weight off before you take range off: a painful range is information, a shortened one is just a worse set.

At 55 minutes, stop where you are

Every session is ordered hardest and most important first, so when the hour's nearly up, cut whatever's left and leave — you've already done the part that matters. On Monday that means the easy cardio goes first; it's on the end of the day for exactly that reason. A session you finish in 55 minutes four weeks running beats a 75-minute one you start skipping in week three.

Food and measuring

Protein: 180 g a day

Roughly 45 g at four meals. One gram per pound of bodyweight. This is the only nutrition number worth tracking right now — it's what keeps the muscle while you lose the fat.

Don't drink calories

Soda, juice, beer, sweet coffee. The easiest few hundred calories a day to lose, and the only food rule in this block besides protein.

Sunday mornings

Weigh in before you eat. Waist at the navel, neck just below the Adam's apple. Same time, same conditions, every week — the trend is the data, not any single number.

No calorie counting yet

If the four-week weight average hasn't moved down by Nov 15, we'll adjust food then. Changing training and food at the same time means learning nothing from either.
